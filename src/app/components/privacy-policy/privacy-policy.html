1. PRIVACY POLICY

TRAFFTAG LLC

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Last Updated: September 30, 2026

TRAFFTAG LLC (“TRAFFTAG,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of our customers, account holders, tag owners, website visitors, and individuals who scan TRAFFTAG QR codes. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how we protect it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to users.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on how you use TRAFFTAG, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, nickname, email address, telephone number, login credentials, account preferences, and other information necessary to create and maintain an account.
  • Tag and Profile Information: Information associated with a TRAFFTAG vehicle, pet, home, item, or LIFE TAG profile that an account holder chooses to provide.
  • QR and Service Activity: Unique QR/tag identifiers, activation status, scan activity, alerts generated through the service, and related service records.
  • Communications: Customer-support communications, service notifications, email or messaging delivery information, and communications submitted to TRAFFTAG.
  • Technical and Security Information: IP address, browser type, device type, timestamps, system logs, authentication records, and security-related activity.
  • Subscription and Transaction Information: Subscription type, transaction history, payment status, and related billing records. Payment-card information may be processed directly by our payment-processing provider rather than stored directly by TRAFFTAG.

LOCATION INFORMATION

NO GPS / PRECISE LOCATION
TRAFFTAG does not request or intentionally collect precise GPS/geolocation information through the QR scanning process. TRAFFTAG may process technical information such as IP addresses, device information, timestamps, and security logs when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, prevent abuse of, and protect the service.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

TRAFFTAG may use information to:

  • Create, activate, operate, maintain, and improve TRAFFTAG accounts and services.
  • Connect QR scans with the appropriate TRAFFTAG tag or account.
  • Generate and deliver authorized notifications and alerts.
  • Manage subscriptions, billing, customer support, and account administration.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Detect spam, fraudulent activity, abusive scanning, unauthorized access, and misuse.
  • Maintain service and security records.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve system performance.
  • Comply with applicable laws, legal obligations, and lawful government requests.
  • Enforce TRAFFTAG’s Terms of Service and other agreements.

SERVICE PROVIDERS AND DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

TRAFFTAG may use service providers to perform functions necessary to operate the service, including hosting and infrastructure, database services, payment processing, email delivery, SMS or messaging services, security, analytics, and customer support. These providers may process information only as necessary to perform services for TRAFFTAG and are subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

TRAFFTAG may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, court orders, subpoenas, or other lawful requests.
  • Protect the rights, property, security, or safety of TRAFFTAG, its users, or others.
  • Detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or illegal activity.
  • Enforce TRAFFTAG agreements.
  • Complete a merger, acquisition, financing, sale, reorganization, or similar business transaction.

TRAFFTAG does not sell personal information in exchange for money.

DATA RETENTION

TRAFFTAG retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing and securing the service, maintaining necessary business and transaction records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud and abuse, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary and no legal or legitimate business reason requires its retention, TRAFFTAG will delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of the information as appropriate.

DATA SECURITY

TRAFFTAG uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information maintained. These measures may include:

  • HTTPS and appropriate encryption.
  • Authentication and password protections.
  • Access controls and database security.
  • Security logging and monitoring.
  • Software and dependency updates.
  • Backup and recovery procedures.

COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

TRAFFTAG may use cookies, session technologies, and similar technologies to operate the website, authenticate users, maintain sessions, remember preferences, prevent fraud, maintain security, and understand website performance.

Where required by applicable law, TRAFFTAG will provide appropriate information and choices concerning non-essential tracking technologies. Any privacy or cookie controls offered by TRAFFTAG are intended to operate as described.

PRIVACY REQUESTS

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information associated with their TRAFFTAG account.

TRAFFTAG may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of a person making a privacy request. Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or other legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

ADULTS AND MINORS

TRAFFTAG accounts are intended to be created and controlled by adults 18 years of age or older. A minor may not independently create or control a TRAFFTAG account.

Where TRAFFTAG permits a LIFE TAG profile for a minor, the profile must be created and managed by the minor’s parent or legal guardian. The parent or legal guardian is responsible for information submitted to the managed profile and for keeping that information accurate and current.

LIFE TAG EMERGENCY DISCLAIMER

LIFE TAG is an informational notification service. LIFE TAG is not a substitute for emergency medical identification, professional medical care, emergency services, law enforcement, or 911.

If there is an emergency, users should contact 911 or the appropriate emergency service immediately.

TRAFFTAG does not guarantee that a QR code will be scanned, that an alert will be delivered or received, or that any person will respond to an alert.

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Last Updated: September 30, 2026

NO MOBILE INFORMATION SHARING

We do not share your mobile phone number or SMS messaging information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

All categories of personal data described in this SMS Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this opt-in information will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates.

MESSAGE FREQUENCY & RATES

When you create a TRAFFTAG account and opt-in to receive vehicle alert notifications, message frequency will vary based on your scanning activity or your assigned vehicle QR tags.

Standard message and data rates may apply for any text messages sent to or received from our system.

OPT-OUT & CUSTOMER ASSISTANCE

You may opt out of receiving text message notifications from TRAFFTAG at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message sent from our service.

For help regarding text messaging, reply HELP to any TRAFFTAG SMS message or contact:
supports@trafftag.com

For privacy-related requests concerning your mobile information, contact:
privacy@trafftag.com

TYPES OF MESSAGES

We do not send unrelated marketing or promotional text messages to your mobile number.

CONSENT

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from TRAFFTAG as described in this policy.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
