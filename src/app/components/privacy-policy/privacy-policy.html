1. PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: [Launch Date] | Last Updated: [Date]

TRAFFTAG LLC ("TRAFFTAG," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of users, customers, tag owners, and people who scan TRAFFTAG QR codes. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information that may be collected, how the information may be used, when it may be disclosed, and choices available to users.

Information We May Collect

  • Account information such as first name, last name, nickname, email address, telephone number, and login/account information.
  • Emergency-contact information supplied by an account holder.
  • Information connected with vehicle, pet, home, item, or LIFE TAG profiles.
  • Unique QR/tag identifiers, activation status, scan history, alerts, finder messages, and related service activity.
  • Device and technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, timestamps, and security logs.
  • Approximate or precise location information when a location feature is enabled and permission is provided where required.
  • Communication information, including service alerts, customer-support communications, and message-delivery records.
  • Subscription and transaction information. Payment-card information may be processed by a payment provider rather than stored directly by TRAFFTAG.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, activate, and improve TRAFFTAG services.
  • Connect a QR scan with the appropriate tag account and deliver authorized notifications.
  • Manage accounts, subscriptions, billing, customer service, and security.
  • Detect spam, fraud, abusive scanning, unauthorized access, and other misuse.
  • Comply with applicable law, lawful requests, and enforce TRAFFTAG agreements.

Service Providers and Disclosures

TRAFFTAG may disclose information to vendors that provide services on its behalf, such as hosting, database, payment processing, email, SMS, messaging, analytics, security, and customer-support services. TRAFFTAG should disclose the actual providers and practices used at launch. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or in connection with a legitimate business transaction.

Location Information

Location information may be used to identify or describe where a scan or reported event occurred. GPS, browser, network, and device location can be inaccurate. TRAFFTAG does not guarantee the accuracy of location information.

Data Retention

TRAFFTAG may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal obligations. Retention periods should be finalized based on the actual production system.

Security

TRAFFTAG will use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information maintained. No online system, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Privacy Requests

Users may contact TRAFFTAG to request access, correction, or deletion of applicable account information, subject to identity verification, legal requirements, fraud prevention, security needs, and legitimate record-retention obligations.

Children

TRAFFTAG accounts are intended to be created by adults age 18 or older. A minor should not independently create an account. Any permitted minor LIFE TAG profile must be created and managed by a parent or legal guardian. Additional parental consent and privacy procedures may be required by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

TRAFFTAG may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes should be communicated as required by law, and the current effective date should be displayed on the website.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through the contact method published on TRAFFTAG.COM. TRAFFTAG should establish dedicated privacy and legal email addresses before launch.

2. CHILDREN & PARENT/GUARDIAN POLICY

Effective Date: [Launch Date] | Last Updated: [Date]

TRAFFTAG accounts are intended for adults age 18 and older. TRAFFTAG may permit an adult parent or legal guardian to create and manage a LIFE TAG profile associated with a minor, subject to the service's rules and applicable law.

Adult Account Requirement

A minor should not independently register for or control a TRAFFTAG account.

Parent/Guardian Responsibility

The parent or legal guardian is responsible for information entered into a minor's managed profile, maintaining account security, deciding what information is appropriate to provide, and keeping the information current.

Minimization

TRAFFTAG should collect only information reasonably necessary to provide the applicable minor-related feature and should avoid publicly displaying sensitive information unless necessary and appropriately protected.

Parental Requests

A verified parent or legal guardian may request applicable access, correction, or deletion of information associated with the managed minor profile, subject to legal and security requirements.

Children Under 13

Before offering functionality directed to children under 13 or knowingly collecting personal information directly from children under 13, TRAFFTAG must implement any parental notice, consent, deletion, and other procedures required by applicable children's privacy law.

Emergency Information

A LIFE TAG is not a substitute for professional medical identification, emergency services, law enforcement, or 911.
