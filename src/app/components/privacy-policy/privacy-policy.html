1. PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Last Updated: September 29, 2026

TRAFFTAG LLC ("TRAFFTAG," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of our customers, account holders, tag owners, website visitors, and individuals who scan TRAFFTAG QR codes.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how we protect it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use TRAFFTAG, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, nickname, email address, telephone number, login credentials, account preferences, and other information necessary to create and maintain an account.
  • Tag and Profile Information: Information associated with a TRAFFTAG vehicle, pet, home, item, or LIFE TAG profile that an account holder chooses to provide.
  • QR and Service Activity: Unique QR/tag identifiers, activation status, scan activity, alerts generated through the service, and related service records.
  • Communications: Customer-support communications, service notifications, email or messaging delivery information, and communications submitted to TRAFFTAG.
  • Technical and Security Information: Information necessary to operate and secure our services, which may include IP address, browser type, device type, timestamps, system logs, authentication records, and security-related activity.
  • Subscription and Transaction Information: Subscription type, transaction history, payment status, and related billing records. Payment-card information may be processed directly by our payment-processing provider rather than stored directly by TRAFFTAG.

Location Information

TRAFFTAG does not request or intentionally collect precise GPS/geolocation information through the QR scanning process. TRAFFTAG may process technical information such as IP addresses, device information, timestamps, and security logs when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, prevent abuse of, and protect the service.

TRAFFTAG does not require users to provide a residential or physical address as part of the standard TRAFFTAG account registration process.

How We Use Information

TRAFFTAG may use information to:

  • Create, activate, operate, maintain, and improve TRAFFTAG accounts and services.
  • Connect QR scans with the appropriate TRAFFTAG tag or account.
  • Generate and deliver authorized notifications and alerts.
  • Manage subscriptions, billing, customer support, and account administration.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Detect spam, fraudulent activity, abusive scanning, unauthorized access, and misuse.
  • Maintain service and security records.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve system performance.
  • Comply with applicable laws, legal obligations, and lawful government requests.
  • Enforce TRAFFTAG's Terms of Service and other agreements.

Service Providers and Disclosure of Information

TRAFFTAG may use service providers to perform functions necessary to operate the service, including hosting and infrastructure, database services, payment processing, email delivery, SMS or messaging services, security, analytics, and customer support. These providers may process information only as necessary to perform services for TRAFFTAG and subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

TRAFFTAG may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, court orders, subpoenas, or other lawful requests.
  • Protect the rights, property, security, or safety of TRAFFTAG, its users, or others.
  • Detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or illegal activity.
  • Enforce TRAFFTAG agreements.
  • Complete a merger, acquisition, financing, sale, reorganization, or similar legitimate business transaction, subject to applicable law.

TRAFFTAG does not sell personal information in exchange for money.

Payment Processing

TRAFFTAG may use a third-party payment processor to process subscription and other payments. TRAFFTAG does not intend to directly store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes when payment information is collected and processed directly by the payment provider. The payment provider's handling of payment information is also governed by its own privacy and security practices.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

TRAFFTAG may use cookies, session technologies, and similar technologies that are reasonably necessary to operate the website, authenticate users, maintain sessions, remember preferences, prevent fraud, maintain security, and understand website performance. Where required by applicable law, TRAFFTAG will provide appropriate information and choices concerning non-essential tracking technologies. Any privacy or cookie controls offered by TRAFFTAG are intended to operate as described.

New York's Attorney General specifically recommends that website privacy controls and disclosures accurately reflect actual tracking practices. — New York State Attorney General

Data Retention

TRAFFTAG retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing and securing the service, maintaining necessary business and transaction records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud and abuse, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal obligations. When information is no longer reasonably necessary and no legal or legitimate business reason requires its retention, TRAFFTAG will delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of the information as appropriate.

Information associated with a deleted account may be retained for a limited period when necessary for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, backup restoration, or other legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

Data Security

TRAFFTAG uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information maintained. These measures may include:

  • HTTPS and appropriate encryption.
  • Authentication and password protections.
  • Access controls.
  • Database and administrative security controls.
  • Security logging and monitoring.
  • Software and dependency updates.
  • Backup and recovery procedures.
  • Restricting employee, developer, contractor, and vendor access according to legitimate business needs.
  • Protecting production credentials and secrets from unauthorized disclosure.

No website, electronic transmission, database, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

New York's SHIELD Act requires covered businesses maintaining private information to implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. — New York State Attorney General

Security Incidents

TRAFFTAG maintains procedures for identifying, investigating, responding to, documenting, and mitigating suspected security incidents. If a security incident results in a breach for which notification is legally required, TRAFFTAG will provide applicable notifications in accordance with applicable law.

Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information associated with their TRAFFTAG account. TRAFFTAG may take reasonable steps to verify the identity and authority of a person making a privacy request. Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary or legally permitted for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or other legitimate recordkeeping purposes. Privacy requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.

Adults and Minors

TRAFFTAG accounts are intended to be created and controlled by adults 18 years of age or older. A minor may not independently create or control a TRAFFTAG account. Where TRAFFTAG permits a LIFE TAG profile for a minor, the profile must be created and managed by the minor's parent or legal guardian. The parent or legal guardian is responsible for information submitted to the managed profile and for keeping that information accurate and current.

TRAFFTAG seeks to limit information associated with a minor to information reasonably necessary for the applicable service. A verified parent or legal guardian may request applicable access, correction, or deletion of information associated with a managed minor profile, subject to applicable legal, security, and record-retention requirements.

Children Under 13

TRAFFTAG does not permit children under 13 to independently create TRAFFTAG accounts. If TRAFFTAG knowingly begins collecting personal information online directly from children under 13 in circumstances covered by COPPA, TRAFFTAG will implement applicable parental notice, verifiable parental consent, parental access/deletion, security, retention, and other requirements before such collection.

The FTC states that COPPA can apply to a general-audience online service when the operator has actual knowledge that it is collecting personal information from a child under 13. — Federal Trade Commission +1

LIFE TAG Emergency Disclaimer

LIFE TAG is an informational notification service. LIFE TAG is not a substitute for emergency medical identification, professional medical care, emergency services, law enforcement, or 911. If there is an emergency, users should contact 911 or the appropriate emergency service immediately. TRAFFTAG does not guarantee that a QR code will be scanned, that an alert will be delivered or received, or that any person will respond to an alert.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

TRAFFTAG may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in its services, technology, information practices, or legal requirements. When appropriate, TRAFFTAG will update the "Last Updated" date above and provide additional notice of material changes when required by applicable law.

2. MOBILE MESSAGING & SMS PRIVACY POLICY (A2P 10DLC COMPLIANCE)

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 | Last Updated: September 12, 2026

TRAFFTAG LLC ("TRAFFTAG," "we," "us," or "our") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your mobile telephone number and SMS messaging information.

No Mobile Information Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of personal data described in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this opt-in information will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates.

Message Frequency & Rates

When you create a TRAFFTAG account and opt-in to receive vehicle alert notifications, message frequency will vary based on user scanning activity on your assigned vehicle QR tags. Standard message and data rates may apply for any text messages sent to or received from our system.

Opt-Out & Customer Assistance

You may opt-out of receiving text message notifications from TRAFFTAG at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message sent from our service. For help or customer support regarding text messaging, reply HELP to any SMS message or contact customer support at supports@trafftag.com.
