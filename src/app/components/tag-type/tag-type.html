A Courtesy Notification Service — Not Ticket Insurance

TRAFFTAG does not guarantee that a vehicle owner will receive, read, or respond to any notification, nor does it guarantee prevention of parking tickets, towing, fines, accidents, or property damage. Vehicle owners remain solely responsible for complying with all applicable parking and traffic laws.
