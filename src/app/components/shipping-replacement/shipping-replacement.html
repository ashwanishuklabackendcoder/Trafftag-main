PHYSICAL TRAFFTAG & ACTIVATION POLICY

Effective Date: [Launch Date] | Last Updated: [Date]

A physical TRAFFTAG may contain a QR code and unique TRAFFTAG identifier intended to connect the physical tag with an account or profile.

Activation

A physical tag may require registration, payment, verification, and activation before the connected services become operational.

Unique Identifiers

Each production tag should use a unique identifier. TRAFFTAG may reject duplicate, invalid, altered, counterfeit, previously activated, or unauthorized identifiers.

Ownership and Transfer

Possession of a physical tag alone does not prove ownership of the associated account. Transfer, reassignment, replacement, or deactivation rules should be provided in the customer's account or applicable product terms.

Placement

Customers are responsible for placing tags in a lawful and reasonably safe location that does not obstruct required visibility, interfere with equipment, or violate vehicle, property, or other applicable rules.

Damage and Replacement

TRAFFTAG does not guarantee that a physical tag will remain readable or attached indefinitely. Replacement and warranty rules should be stated at purchase.

Counterfeiting and Misuse

Users may not counterfeit, maliciously duplicate, alter, replace, or use TRAFFTAG identifiers to impersonate another account or interfere with the service.
