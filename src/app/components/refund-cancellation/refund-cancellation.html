SUBSCRIPTION, BILLING, CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: [Launch Date] | Last Updated: [Date]

This policy explains the general rules for paid TRAFFTAG subscriptions and physical tags. The checkout page must display the actual price, billing frequency, included services, renewal terms, taxes, and material restrictions before the customer completes payment.

Subscription Plans

TRAFFTAG may offer monthly, annual, or other subscription plans. Each plan may include a specified number or type of alerts or communication channels. The exact plan purchased controls.

Billing Authorization

By purchasing a recurring subscription, the customer authorizes the selected payment method to be charged according to the terms disclosed at checkout, until the subscription is canceled or otherwise ends.

Automatic Renewal

If a plan renews automatically, the renewal frequency and cancellation method must be clearly disclosed before purchase and implemented in accordance with applicable law.

Alert Allowances

If a plan includes a limited number of alerts, TRAFFTAG may stop, restrict, or separately charge for additional alerts only as disclosed in the applicable plan terms. The production website should clearly explain what occurs when the allowance is exhausted.

Failed Payments

TRAFFTAG may retry a failed payment, request an updated payment method, restrict paid functionality, or suspend the subscription, consistent with the terms disclosed to the customer.

Cancellation

Customers may cancel through the cancellation method provided by TRAFFTAG. The website must clearly state whether cancellation is immediate or effective at the end of the current paid billing period.

Refunds

Refund eligibility, including treatment of partial billing periods, annual subscriptions, unused alerts, physical tags, shipping charges, duplicate payments, and erroneous charges, must be clearly disclosed before purchase. Any no-refund rule is subject to applicable law.

Price Changes

TRAFFTAG may change future prices. Recurring customers should receive any notice required by applicable law before a new price applies.
