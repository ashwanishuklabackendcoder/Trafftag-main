This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the rules for paid TRAFFTAG subscriptions and physical tags (stickers). By purchasing a product or service from TRAFFTAG, you agree to the terms in this policy.

SUBSCRIPTION PLANS

TRAFFTAG may offer monthly, annual, or other subscription plans. Each plan may include a specified number or type of alerts or communication channels (Email, SMS, etc.). The actual price, billing frequency, included services, alert allowance, renewal terms, taxes, and any applicable restrictions will be clearly displayed before you complete your purchase.

BILLING AUTHORIZATION

By purchasing a recurring subscription, you authorize TRAFFTAG and its payment processor to charge the selected payment method according to the terms presented at checkout. This authorization continues until your subscription is canceled or otherwise ends.

AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

If your plan is set to automatically renew, the renewal frequency and applicable price will be clearly disclosed before purchase. Your subscription will continue to renew automatically until you cancel it in accordance with this policy.

ALERT ALLOWANCES

If a plan includes a limited number of alerts, unused alerts do not roll over to the next billing period unless TRAFFTAG expressly states otherwise for that plan. When the included alert allowance is exhausted, additional alerts will be unavailable or handled according to the terms of the purchased plan.

FAILED PAYMENTS

If a payment fails, TRAFFTAG may retry the payment, request an updated payment method, restrict access to paid functionality, or suspend the subscription until payment is successfully completed.

CANCELLATION

You may cancel your subscription at any time using the cancellation method provided by TRAFFTAG. Cancellation stops future renewal charges. You may continue to use the paid service through the end of your current paid billing period, unless otherwise stated at the time of cancellation or required by applicable law.

DIGITAL SUBSCRIPTION REFUNDS

Except where required by applicable law or expressly stated otherwise at purchase, subscription charges for an activated billing period are non-refundable. Unused alerts remaining at cancellation or expiration have no cash value and are not refundable.

PHYSICAL TAGS & STICKERS

If a physical TRAFFTAG product arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and supporting information (such as photos). We will review the issue and provide a replacement or refund in accordance with our Shipping Policy and applicable law.

DUPLICATE OR INCORRECT CHARGES

If you believe you were charged more than once or charged incorrectly, please contact us so we can investigate and correct the issue when appropriate.

PRICE CHANGES

TRAFFTAG may change prices for future billing periods. If a price change affects your recurring subscription, we will provide any notice required by applicable law before the new price takes effect.
