About TRAFFTAG

Privacy-first QR alerts for everything you own — vehicles, pets, homes and belongings.

Who We Are

TRAFFTAG was founded to solve a simple problem: people need a private way to reach an owner about an urgent issue — a blocked car, a lost pet, an unlocked gate — without exposing anyone's personal phone number.

Traditional methods, like handwritten notes or visible phone numbers, expose owners to spam, harassment, and identity theft. TRAFFTAG replaces all of that with a single, encrypted QR decal.

How TRAFFTAG Works

Every TRAFFTAG decal has a unique, tamper-evident QR code linked securely to the owner's account.

Anyone who scans it with a smartphone camera can send a pre-formatted or custom alert — a blocked driveway, a lost pet, a package at the door, lights left on — without ever seeing the owner's phone number or personal identity.
